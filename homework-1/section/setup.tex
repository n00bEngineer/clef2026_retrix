\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}
All experiments were conducted on our machines, utilizing a computer with an AMD Ryzen 9 5900X, 32 GB RAM and NVIDIA RTX 3090 GPU (10496 CUDA Cores, boost clock 1.70 GHz, 24 GB of GDDR6X memory, 35.58 TFlops) for resource-intensive workload and our laptops for standard workloads and refining of the programs. We have four laptops, the two most powerful are a MacBook Air M3 and one with no dedicated GPU, 16GB RAM and a Intel core i7-13700H. 
For reproducibility, all the environment settings have been documented and are freely available or easily obtained by following the instructions in this paper.
